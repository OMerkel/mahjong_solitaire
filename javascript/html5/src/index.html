Rules

Mahjong Solitaire is a single-player tile matching game played on a layered layout.

Goal

Remove all 144 tiles by matching legal pairs.

Legal Pair
  1. Both tiles must be free: no tile directly on top and at least one side (left or right) open.
  2. Tiles must belong to the same matching group.
  3. Flowers match any flower. Seasons match any season.
When Is a Tile Blocked?

A tile is blocked and cannot be selected if any of the following apply:

  • Covered from above: Another tile sits on top of it — even partially. If any part of a tile's surface is overlapped by a tile on a higher layer, it is blocked.
  • Squeezed on both sides: Tiles at the same level that are fully aligned block their left and right neighbors in the classic way. A tile is blocked when it has a direct neighbor on both its left and right side.
  • Nudged from the side: Some tiles are placed slightly offset (half a tile up or down). These offset tiles reach into the space of their aligned neighbors. Any aligned tile that an offset tile overlaps from the side is blocked by it — but the offset tile itself is only blocked by tiles further out from the center.

In short: a tile is free only when nothing covers it from above and it has at least one completely open side.

Layouts

Default layout is Classic Turtle. Additional layouts: Heart and Square.
